# MindChat Privacy Policy

Effective date: November 24, 2026

## 1. Overview

MindChat is a browser extension designed to help users organize, visualize, and navigate ChatGPT conversations through a branching tree structure.

## 2. Data Access and Usage

MindChat accesses the following information solely to provide its functionality:

- ChatGPT conversation URLs and identifiers.
- Parent-child relationships between conversations.
- User-created descriptions and preferences.
- Conversation content used to generate previews.

MindChat does not request access to passwords, payment information, or authentication credentials. Conversation content may contain personal or sensitive information entered by users.

## 3. Data Storage and Synchronization

MindChat stores conversation tree information, preferences, and cached previews using browser-provided storage.

Conversation tree metadata and supported preferences may be synchronized across devices through the browser's built-in synchronization service.

Conversation previews are cached locally on the user's device.

MindChat does not operate an external server for collecting or storing conversation data.

## 4. Data Sharing

MindChat does not sell user data, use it for advertising, or transfer it to third parties for unrelated purposes.

Browser-provided synchronization is subject to the privacy policies of the respective browser provider.

## 5. Browser Permissions

MindChat uses browser permissions to store extension data, interact with ChatGPT tabs, detect conversation navigation, and maintain data synchronization.

Host access is restricted to chatgpt.com.

## 6. User Control

Users can disable conversation previews, manage browser synchronization settings, and remove MindChat through their browser's extension settings.

Users can clear locally stored extension data through applicable browser controls. Synchronized data may be subject to separate browser account controls.

## 7. Changes to This Policy

This privacy policy may be updated to reflect changes in MindChat's functionality or data-handling practices.
